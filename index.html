Institutional Identity & Ethos

WHERE CURIOSITY MEETS INNOVATION

INFINITY – The Tech Society of Sri Guru Gobind Singh College of Commerce, University of Delhi, is a student-led collective committed to democratizing technology, creative design, and ethical computational awareness.

01

Our Story

Formed at Sri Guru Gobind Singh College of Commerce (Pitampura, New Delhi), INFINITY brings together curious minds across Commerce, Economics, and Computer Science to collaborate on technology, creativity, and practical problem-solving.

02

Our Mission

To encourage students to explore emerging technologies, build practical skills, collaborate on high-impact projects, and cultivate responsible technological leadership for tomorrow's digital economy.

03

Our Vision

To create an inclusive collegiate environment where curiosity, experimentation, and technological growth have no boundaries—believing firmly that ideas can evolve into infinite possibilities.

Recruitment Requirements

Selection criteria and eligibility guidelines

  • Open to All Courses: B.Sc (H) CS, B.Com (H), BMS, BBE, B.A. (H) Econ, and all SGGSCC students across 1st and 2nd years.
  • Curiosity First: Prior coding experience is helpful for technical roles, but curiosity and willingness to learn matter most.
  • Domain Passion: Strong interest in at least one of our 5 domains (AI, Cybersecurity, Esports, Design, Community).
  • Commitment: Availability for weekly syncs, event preparation sprints, and workshop support.
Official Disclaimer: Final selection criteria, interview schedules, and induction rounds must be confirmed by the society's official notice.
